**DEC-57 rule (client meeting 19 Nov, item 3, as clarified by the owner on 20 Nov)**
- Admin: everything.
- Supervisor: worksites assigned in `worksite_supervisors`, and the workers on job items at those worksites. Their own crew stays visible only on Employees, Leave and their own requests (they file those); not on Worksites, the planner job-item picker, Attendance or the Live Map. Attendance and live position on another supervisor's site belong to that site's supervisor (read-only, may comment).
- Superintendent / Engineer: worksites assigned to any supervisor in their reporting scope; the workers in their scope plus the workers on those worksites.
- A worker from another crew is read-only on the supervisor's worksite. Approvals stay with the crew chain (DEC-10).
- Same rule on every scoped list: Worksites, planner picker, Employees, Attendance, Live Map, Pending Visits, Reports.
- Fix for the empty lists: a Worksite_Supervisors import sheet, an Admin action "Assign supervisors from current crews" (propose, review, confirm; never automatic), and an "Unassigned worksites" count and filter.
