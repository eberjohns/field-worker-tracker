## Full notice

**Who we are.** [COMPANY NAME], [ADDRESS]. Contact for questions about your data: [NAME / EMAIL / PHONE].

**Why we use your location.** To record where you worked and for how long, to pay you correctly (including overtime), to check that work at each site was done, and to keep workers safe and the schedule accurate.

**When we collect it.** From when you stop the morning alarm until your working day ends. We do not track you outside working time.

**What we collect.**
- Your phone's location (latitude, longitude, accuracy) and the time, when you arrive at or leave a work site or accommodation, at regular checks while you are at a site, and when you switch location on or off.
- Information about your phone needed to keep your account safe (device model, system version, app version).
- Your name, worker ID, schedule, attendance and pay information held in the system.

**What we do not collect.** Your messages, photos, contacts, calls or location outside working time.

**Who can see it.** Your supervisor, superintendent, engineer and authorised administrators, only as needed for their work. Pay information is visible only to administrators and engineers responsible for you. Our technology providers process data for us under contract: [HOSTING PROVIDER], [PUSH/EMAIL PROVIDERS]. We do not sell your data.

**Where it is stored and for how long.** Stored on secure servers in [COUNTRY]. Location records are kept for [X months] and then archived/deleted; attendance and payroll records are kept for [Y years] as required by labour law and for payroll disputes.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see your data, ask us to correct it, ask questions about how it is used, and withdraw your consent. Ask your supervisor or contact [CONTACT]. We answer within [30] days. If you withdraw consent, location tracking stops; we will tell you how this affects recording of your attendance.

**Security.** Your data is protected with encryption and restricted access, and every access to location data is logged.

**Changes.** If this notice changes, the app will ask you to read and accept it again.

**Complaints.** You can contact [COMPANY CONTACT] or the data protection authority in [UAE: UAE Data Office / KSA: SDAIA].
